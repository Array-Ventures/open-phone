Connect an iPhone

Unlock your iPhone, connect its USB data cable and trust this Mac. Pair Bluetooth and enable AssistiveTouch.

Settings → General → About → Bluetooth. With several phones, check that the USB screen and address belong to the same phone.

Phone controls

Home requires AssistiveTouch device Button 3 assigned to Home.

Clipboard, apps and URLs

Requires the OpenPhone Shortcut bridge and device Button 9 assigned to your imported Shortcut. Approve its first-run permissions on your phone.

After copying, focus a phone text field and paste. Your phone may ask to allow pasting from Shortcuts.
